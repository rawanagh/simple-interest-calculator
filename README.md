Simple Interest Calculator

Description

A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned on a principal amount over a specific period of time.

Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Inputs

* Principal: The initial amount of money.
* Rate: The annual interest rate.
* Time: The time period in years.

Output

The calculator displays the total simple interest.

Example

Principal = 1000

Rate = 5%

Time = 2 years

Simple Interest = 100
